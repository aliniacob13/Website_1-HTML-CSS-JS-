JUSTIN HURWITZ - The mind behind one of the best soundtracks ever made

Justin Hurwitz is an American film composer and a television writer. He is best known for his longtime collaboration with director Damien Chazelle, scoring each of his films: Guy and Madeline on a Park Bench (2009), Whiplash (2014), La La Land (2016), First Man (2018), and Babylon (2022).
The songs and score for La La Land were composed and orchestrated by Justin Hurwitz, Chazelle's Harvard University classmate, who also worked on his two prior films. The lyrics were written by Pasek and Paul, except for "Start a Fire", which was written by John Legend, Hurwitz, Marius de Vries and Angelique Cinelu. A soundtrack album was released on December 9, 2016, by Interscope Records, featuring selections from Hurwitz's score and songs performed by the cast.
The film's opening number, "Another Day of Sun", shot as a single tracking shot on an L.A. freeway, received praise for its choreography, while the songs "City of Stars" and "Audition (The Fools Who Dream)" received numerous awards.
The creative process for a film often begins with music, drawing from a mix of sources—sometimes original compositions or a wide-ranging playlist that inspires the initial stages of writing.
This collection of music can shape the tone and atmosphere, creating an early "soundscape" that sets the direction for the film's development. For La La Land, music was crafted even before the script, functioning almost like a treatment. In this case, the music was integrated from the earliest stages, though typically, the composer receives the full script first.
From there, they break down scenes, identifying where music will be featured and establishing initial themes and sounds with virtual instruments. This phase leads into storyboarding and refining each musical element before recording, shaping the final sound of the film.
